# Community Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants must make this a respectful, technically rigorous, and safe place to
collaborate. Helpful behavior includes assuming good intent while evaluating evidence,
welcoming questions, explaining proof limits, respecting privacy, giving actionable
review feedback, and correcting mistakes without humiliation.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
content, personal attacks, sustained disruption, doxxing, publishing private source or
artifacts without authority, deliberate misrepresentation of evidence, or retaliation
against a good-faith reporter.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, reviews,
release channels, and project-representative interactions. It applies to maintainers as
well as contributors.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not report conduct incidents in a public issue. A private conduct-reporting channel
has not yet been configured because the public repository does not exist. Public issue,
pull-request, and discussion intake must remain disabled until the maintainer publishes
and tests a private conduct contact in this section. Absence of that contact is a
release blocker, not an invitation to use the security channel for non-security
matters.

Once active, reports should identify the behavior, approximate time, project surface,
and any witnesses or links needed for review. Do not collect or send unrelated personal
data. The maintainer will limit disclosure to what is reasonably necessary to assess
and respond, but cannot promise legal privilege or absolute confidentiality.

Depending on severity and pattern, proportionate responses include a private
correction, content removal, warning, temporary participation restriction, permanent
ban, or referral to the hosting provider or appropriate authorities. Immediate threats,
doxxing, and severe harassment may skip intermediate steps. Conflicts involving the
sole maintainer must be escalated through the hosting provider until independent
maintainers exist.

This project-specific policy is intentionally concise. It creates behavioral and
enforcement expectations without claiming an operational private channel before one
exists.
